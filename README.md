# Inayat Electronics - Official Business Website

Modern, premium, and professional business website for **Inayat Electronics** — an electrical and solar solutions business based in Pakistan providing electrical supplies, home wiring, fault finding, fan repairs, LED lighting, and solar power installations.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Split Hero Section**: High-impact introduction with trust indicators and professional electrical panel imagery.
- **Our Electrical Services**: 6 dedicated service cards with line icons and direct WhatsApp inquiries.
- **Product Showcase**: 11 electrical & solar products with interactive category filtering (Wiring, Supplies, Lighting & Fans, Solar Energy).
- **Solar Solutions Focus**: Deep charcoal high-contrast section highlighting solar system installation, hybrid inverters, and battery storage.
- **Safety & Wiring Spotlight**: Focus on proper load distribution, circuit breaker safety, and neat conduit finishes.
- **Why Choose Us**: 8-point trust and reliability grid.
- **4-Step Workflow**: Transparent step-by-step service process.
- **Accordion FAQ**: 8 frequently asked questions with accessible interactive accordions.
- **Centralized WhatsApp Integration**: Dynamically routes inquiries with pre-filled messages to the business number across all CTAs.
- **Local SEO & Schema Markup**: Optimized with `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD structured data and semantic HTML5.
